Christian Prayer Journal

This is a simple Christian prayer journal website designed to help users organize their prayer time, reflect on their faith, and grow closer to God.

Features: 

- Home: A welcome section with a space to enter the date.
- Prayer Requests: Write down personal prayer requests.
- Gratitude List: Keep track of things to be thankful for.
- Praise & Worship: Express praise and gratitude to God.
- Repentance & Forgiveness: A space for confession and personal reflection.
- Verse of the Day: Read a Bible verse and reflect on its meaning.
- Prayers for Others: Write prayers for family, friends, and others.
- My Notes: Record personal thoughts and reflections.

The website also includes a navigation sidebar and completion checkboxes to help users organize their journaling.

Technologies Used: 
- HTML5
- CSS3

This project was created to practice web development while combining my interest in coding with my Christian faith.
